Dammit. 

I recently got into audiobooks. I'm from Poland and I prefer to listen to them in polish. For me, THE app for audiobooks is https://audioteka.com/pl/ (they didn't pay me btw.). 

There's just one thing that I don't like about it, and it is a deal breaker for me. But first I have to explain their subscription model.

Audioteka offers a few plans. **Audioteka Plus** - you pay 25 zł/mo and get 1 point to spend on any audiobook, which you can download as mp3 - great and I love it. 

**Audioteka Klub** - you pray 30 zł/mo to get access to a limited set of audiobooks, that you can listen in app only - which is totally fine. I get that they don't want people to download their whole library.

**Audioteka Klub** has one more thing to offer, if you have the subscription, you can pay 23 zł for any book ( which is simmilar to what **Audioteka Plus** offers). BUT there's a catch. You cannot get an mp3 file for that book - which grinds my gears. 

I paid for subscription, and a premium on top of that to get a book and I cannot download it ahhhh... 

One last thing. The ultimate fuck you, which all the subscriptions companies can pull off -  [revoke the access to the content that YOU bought. Anytime](https://web.audioteka.com/legal/pl/Regulamin-Audioteka-Klub.pdf). Imagine that you pay for a thing and they take it from you :) Because in the end - you never had it. It was safely behind their app and its DRM :) Oh and If this company goes under, your audiobooks go under with it.

Ok. Maybe there's a way to get more of these **Audioteka Plus** "points" which would totally solve my problem. But NO. I can get a "voucher" for 40 zł. I want to pay a fair price for your service, and I hate that the only solution seems to be to create multiple accounts.
